About me

Hi, I’m Maanya.

I'm a Computer Science student who enjoys building products at the intersection of AI, software engineering, and impact. What started as curiosity about how technology works has evolved into a passion for creating systems that solve real problems one of which being how to make learning more accessible.

Lately, I've been exploring large language models, retrieval systems, AI workflows, backend development, and developer tools. I enjoy understanding how things work under the hood—whether that's training and fine-tuning models, designing scalable systems, or shipping applications that people actually use.

Outside of coding, I spend a lot of time teaching and working on education initiatives. Those experiences have shaped how I think about technology: the best products aren't just technically impressive—they're intuitive, inclusive, and genuinely useful.

I'm always building something, learning something new, or breaking something while trying to understand it. Right now, I'm focused on becoming an engineer who can bridge research, product, and people—not just write code, but build technology that creates meaningful impact.

Beyond tech, I have an extensive list of hobbies. For my creative side, I crochet, Paint, Read and Write. I love being outdoors and spend time Surfing, Running, Swimming, Skateboarding and Hiking. I play the ukulele sometimes and I've been a kathak dancer for over a decade :)
